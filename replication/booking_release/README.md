# Reserved bookings and short-notice reassignment

Run `python check_appointment_release.py`. The standard-library script uses exact fractions, checks conservation of time, both sharp endpoints and the discrete marginal effect of one additional booked unit. The 11,979 configurations are deterministic tests of identities under specified assumptions. The illustrative clinic example is synthetic. A unit unused by these two cohorts may be used for other work.

The mechanism is motivated by the [Royal Free London post-fitting service](https://www.royalfree.nhs.uk/patients-and-visitors/patient-information-leaflets/telephone-follow) and [national short-notice list guidance](https://www.england.nhs.uk/2023/01/nhs-drive-to-reduce-no-shows-to-help-tackle-long-waits-for-care/). Neither reports the attendance or reassignment fractions used in the illustration.
