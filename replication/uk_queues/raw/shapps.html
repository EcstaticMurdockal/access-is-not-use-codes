Select Committee on Health Written Evidence

Evidence submitted by Grant Shapps MP (AUDIO 30)

  I have recently carried out research into audiology provision, in particular the waiting times for fitting of hearing aids, which was an issue brought up by some of my constituents. With around two million people using or needing the use of hearing aids this is a very important issue as far as health services are concerned.

  I noticed the Health Committee is carrying out a short inquiry into audiology services, I hope this information is of some use to you.

Grant Shapps MP

8 February 2007
